How a project works

No surprises. You'll know the price and the plan before we start.

  1. Walkthrough

    We visit, listen, measure and talk through budget and timing.

  2. Written estimate

    A line-by-line price and schedule, usually within a week.

  3. Build

    Permits pulled, work done, and regular updates so you're never guessing.

  4. Final walkthrough

    We go over every detail together and back the work with a warranty.
