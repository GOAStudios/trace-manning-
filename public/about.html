About us

A local, owner-run contractor. When you call, you talk to the person who'll be on your job.

Built on showing up

[Owner name] started Hartwell Construction in 2008 after ten years framing and finishing homes for other builders. The idea was simple: do the work right, keep customers in the loop, and leave every job site cleaner than we found it.

Today we're a small crew of carpenters plus licensed electricians, plumbers and HVAC partners we've worked with for years. We take on a limited number of projects at a time so the owner can be on site for every one.

Most of our work comes from referrals and repeat customers, and we'd like to keep it that way.
