About us

A local, owner-run contractor. When you call, you talk to Trace.

A builder who knows the numbers

Trace Manning has spent his career building homes and pricing them. That's why Manning General Contractors offers three things: we build, we estimate, and we consult.

On construction jobs, we're a small crew plus licensed electricians, plumbers and HVAC partners we've worked with for years. We take on a limited number of projects so Trace can be on site for every one.

On estimating and consulting work, homeowners get the same eye for cost and quality, whether we're building the project or checking someone else's bid.

Most of our work comes from referrals and repeat customers, and we'd like to keep it that way.
