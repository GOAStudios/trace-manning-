Frequently asked questions

Don't see your question? Call and ask. You'll talk to Trace directly.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We're a licensed Florida contractor (license #GC-000000) and carry general liability and workers' comp insurance. We're happy to send proof before any work starts.

Do you charge for estimates?

Walkthroughs and estimates for projects we build are free. Detailed estimates, takeoffs and consulting for projects someone else will build are paid services, and we quote the fee up front.

Can you review a bid I got from another contractor?

Yes. We'll check it for missing items, unclear allowances and prices that look high or low, and give you questions to ask before you sign.

What does owner representation mean?

We act as your construction expert during someone else's project: reviewing proposals, checking progress and pay requests, and looking over change orders.

Can you do a material takeoff from my plans?

Yes. Send your plans and we'll return an itemized material list you can use to price or order.

How long does an estimate take?

Usually within a week of the walkthrough. Bigger projects with plans can take a little longer.

Do you pull the permits?

Yes. We handle permits and inspections for every job that needs them.

How does payment work?

A deposit to get on the schedule, then payments tied to clear milestones. You'll never pay for work that isn't done.

How long will my project take?

It depends on the job. A bathroom remodel is often 2–4 weeks and an addition 2–4 months. Your estimate includes a schedule.

Do you work with insurance claims?

Yes. For storm and water damage, we can document the damage and work with your adjuster.

Is your work built for Florida weather?

Yes. Everything we build meets the current Florida Building Code, including wind-load requirements.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes, a 2-year warranty on our workmanship, plus the manufacturer warranties on materials.
